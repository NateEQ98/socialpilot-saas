A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Everything clear before you create.

Simple answers, no inflated claims or hidden surprises.

Explore the free tools ↗
What can I use without paying?+

You can explore content ideas, draft captions, and use the planner’s free features. Some saved features require a free account. AI campaign and image generation require a Pro subscription.

Does SocialPilot publish to Instagram or TikTok for me?+

No. SocialPilot helps you prepare content and plan your schedule, but it does not currently connect to social accounts to publish posts automatically.

What does SocialPilot Pro include?+

Pro includes AI campaign generation and AI image creation alongside the existing creative workspace. Image generation is limited to up to five images per customer per UTC day, subject to shared service capacity.

How much is Pro, and can I cancel?+

Pro is $19.99 USD per month and renews automatically until canceled. You can manage or cancel through Stripe billing. Cancellation ordinarily stops the next renewal, with access continuing through the current paid period. Review our Terms of Service for billing and refund information.

Are the campaign examples real customer results?+

No. Landing-page previews and example posts are illustrative. Real AI-generated campaigns are created in Pro Studio. We never present sample engagement numbers as actual customer performance.
