Privacy Policy

How Ariavora collects, uses, stores, and protects information.

Effective November 24, 2026 · Last updated November 24, 2026

In brief: Ariavora uses account information to run your workspace, uses Stripe to handle payments, and sends prompts to AI providers when you request generated content. Avoid entering sensitive personal information into AI prompts.

1. Information we collect

When you register, we process your email address, authentication identifiers, and account status. When you use the tools, we process content you submit, such as brand descriptions, text prompts, campaign drafts, generated images you choose to save, calendar entries, and related usage records. We also process subscription identifiers and plan status for billing and access management.

Our service providers may collect technical data necessary to deliver and secure their services, such as IP addresses, device/browser information, timestamps, and security logs. Ariavora does not need your full payment-card number to manage your subscription.

2. How we use information

  • Authenticate users and maintain their accounts.
  • Save and display content calendars, campaigns, and images when requested.
  • Generate AI campaigns and images from the information users provide.
  • Verify subscription status, enforce usage allowances, and prevent abuse.
  • Process payments, support billing, maintain security, and comply with legal obligations.

3. Providers and sharing

Ariavora uses Supabase for authentication and hosted account or content data; Stripe for checkout, subscription management, and payment processing; and an AI generation provider for campaign or image generation. These companies may process information as needed to provide their services, under their respective agreements and privacy terms. Third-party stock photos, videos, and other linked content may load from their own providers.

We do not publish your private drafts as customer testimonials or share your full card details through the Ariavora website. We may disclose information when legally required or necessary to protect users and the service.

4. AI processing

When you generate a campaign or image, the relevant text prompt, brand/product details, creative settings, and necessary processing data are sent to the AI service. Do not include passwords, payment-card details, health records, confidential client information, or other sensitive data in prompts. AI results may contain errors or unintended content and require your review.

5. Browser data and storage

The site uses browser storage for some workspace features, such as local planning drafts, and authentication technologies supplied by Supabase to maintain a session. These technologies are necessary for the requested functionality. Stripe and other providers may use cookies and related technologies on their own pages under their privacy notices.

6. Retention and deletion

We keep information while needed to provide accounts, saved content, subscriptions, security protections, and applicable record-keeping. You can delete certain saved campaigns or images through the interface. Closing a billing subscription does not automatically delete account data. Some records may be retained for legal, fraud-prevention, transaction, or security purposes. To request access, correction, or deletion of account information, use the support route provided with your billing communications until a dedicated support contact is published.

7. Security and international processing

We use access controls and provider security features designed to protect account and content data, although no online service can guarantee perfect security. Providers may process information in countries other than your own, subject to applicable law and their contractual safeguards.

8. Your privacy choices

You can stop using the service, remove supported saved content, cancel recurring charges through the Stripe billing portal, or request assistance concerning your personal information. Depending on where you live, privacy laws may give you additional rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain information about the processing of your data. We will honor applicable rights after appropriate verification.

9. Children

Ariavora subscriptions are intended for adults aged 18 and older and are not designed for children. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information directly from children.

10. Updates and contact

We may update this policy as features and providers change. The revision date appears above. For privacy requests or questions, email support@ariavora.com.
